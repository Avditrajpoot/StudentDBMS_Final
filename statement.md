# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Educational institutions need a structured way to maintain student, course and enrollment records. Manual or disconnected records make CRUD operations, relationship management and summary reporting harder to maintain.

## Scope
The system stores students, courses and enrollments in a relational MySQL database. It supports student CRUD, course creation/listing, enrollment, grade updates and basic reports.

## Target Users
- Academic administrators
- Faculty or course coordinators
- Students' records staff

## High-Level Features
- Student registration and maintenance
- Course management
- Student-course enrollment
- Grade recording
- Dashboard and department reports
- Relational integrity and validation
